A small shop, run by one person.

Alex Store is an independent shop, not a marketplace. There is no third-party seller between you and the person who picks and packs your order, and no checkout queue — ordering is a WhatsApp conversation.

How ordering works

  1. 1

    Find what you want

    Browse the catalog, or search by meaning — describing what you need in your own words works better than guessing keywords.

  2. 2

    Message us on WhatsApp

    Tap Buy on WhatsApp and the product, its price and its description are already written into the message. Nothing is sent anywhere except to our number.

  3. 3

    We confirm and arrange delivery

    You get a reply from a person, usually within a few hours. We confirm availability, agree a delivery time and take payment however is easiest for you.

Why you can buy with confidence

You deal with a person

There is no automated support queue and no third-party seller. Your message reaches the shop, and the reply comes from the shop.

Prices are plain rupees

Everything is listed in PKR at a fixed price. There is no currency conversion, no checkout surprise and no payment page collecting card details.

Descriptions are specifications

Each product carries one short factual line — material, capacity, care instructions. It is written to tell you whether the thing suits you, not to sell it to you.

Availability is stated honestly

Sold-out products say Sold out rather than staying in the catalog pretending to be purchasable.

A deliberately small range

We stock a short, edited list rather than everything. It means each piece we list is one we would happily use ourselves, and we can tell you honestly what it is actually like.

Free delivery over the threshold

Orders above the stated threshold ship free. Claim the free-delivery voucher the first time you visit, and the figure stays the same between checkout and delivery.

Refunds and cancellations

  • Cancel any time before your order is dispatched — free, no questions asked.
  • Once an order has been dispatched, a cancellation fee applies. The fee covers the return shipping and the handling, and it is deducted from your refund.
  • If something arrives damaged, or it isn’t what you ordered, message us within 7 days of delivery and we’ll replace it or refund the full amount, including delivery.
  • Refunds go back the same way you paid, within 3 working days of the item reaching us.
  • Items need to be unused and in the condition you received them.

Every product page and the Android app carry a short version of this. The full policy is the one on this page.
